## the honest story

I picked up coding at **11**. Not because someone told me to — because I was curious and the internet was right there.

I learned a little. I got stuck. I quit.  
I came back. Learned more. Got stuck again. Quit again.  
Came back again.

That's been my relationship with programming — **on and off, but never over**. Every time I stepped away, something pulled me back in. An idea I couldn't build yet. A problem I couldn't solve. The memory of the one time something I made actually *worked*.

Along the way I've learned from brilliant people — open source contributors, indie hackers, strangers on YouTube who explained things clearly, Twitter threads at 2am. I owe a lot to the internet.

Right now I'm 17, deep in full-stack development, and shipping real things.
